Images for the landing (sign-in) page.

  landing-photo.jpg     picture for the left panel: Gujarat map with activities (1254 x 940, shown whole).

  logos/emblem.png      State Emblem, as used by the Government of Gujarat (from sycd.gujarat.gov.in).
                        Used for the department and for every office without its own logo.
  logos/SAG.png         Sports Authority of Gujarat (supplied by the user)
  logos/GSA.png         Gujarat Sahitya Akademi (supplied by the user)

To add another office's own logo (CYSCA, ARCH, MUS): add logos/<ID>.png and list it in FILES in js/logos.js.
Logos are shown inside a white circle, so a square image with the logo centred works best.
